# Phase 27 visual QA findings

- Preview desktop at 1440px opened `/?workspace=opportunity-feed` directly and showed the Live Opportunity Feed header, Market State, display-only filter group, honest loading state, and owner-authentication gate for private Auto Paper evidence.
